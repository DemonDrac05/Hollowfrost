# Vertical Slice

## Goal
Prove two things: the game is worth playing, and the technology holds up.
2–3 hours of play, every core system present at minimum depth.

## Content
- One region: Nordic forest and the old village, about 2 × 2 km.
- Opening cutscene: landing, the road to the village, the massacre.
- Survive and evade: early creatures far stronger than the player.
- Village ruins and infected villagers.
- The chief's house: his diary, the first evidence of the experiments.
- A fallen friend with a note and a family photo, then the boss reveal.
- First boss: the mutated village chief, still conscious.

## Systems (one of each)
- Survival: hunger, thirst, temperature, wet clothing, 3 status effects.
- Combat: 1 melee weapon, 1 gun with scarce ammo, 2 enemy types, 1 boss.
- Crafting: about 10 recipes. Building: about 8 pieces
  (foundation, wall, door, roof, campfire, bed, chest, planting bed).
- Serums: 1 cure, 1 permanent enhancement.
- Evidence: about 10 documents/recordings, the evidence board.
- World: one season playable, time-skip debug command for the others.
- Co-op: 2–4 players, downed and revive. Death and recovery.
- Stealth and noise; assassination; parry and finishers; infected meat and its cure plant.

## Out of scope
Other regions and climates, endings, more serums, structural integrity, vehicles.
The western quarry and everything beyond the first boss.
Anything else goes to the "later" list in VISION.md.

## Done when
- Two players finish the slice together: ThinkPad as host, HP as client.
- HP holds 30 FPS on Very Low for 30 minutes, plugged in, without hitches.
- People who did not make the game play it and want to keep going.
